## Owner confirmation — 2026-10-02

The project owner confirmed ownership of the supplied references and identified
ChatGPT as the tool that generated the logo, boot storyboard, cave artwork and
system icons at their request. The project's existing owner-directed MIT grant
therefore includes these image outputs and the checked-in derivatives. This
resolves the missing provenance record; no images were replaced.

[OpenAI Terms of Use](https://openai.com/policies/terms-of-use/), effective
2026-01-01, assign OpenAI's rights in output to the user to the extent permitted
by law. This records the distribution basis; it does not claim that AI output
has exclusive copyright or license third-party material.

The exact image model/version and original reference sheets were not retained.
The introducing commits and preparation prompts remain the generation record.
[Current asset hashes](PROVENANCE.sha256) identify the specific checked-in SVG/PNG
files covered by this record. Regenerate the hash list when an asset changes.
